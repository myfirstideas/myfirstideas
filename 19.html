How the Patient Score Works

At RatedByPatients, we rank telehealth and telemedicine providers using a transparent scoring system built on patient feedback and measurable criteria.

What Goes Into Your Score

Our Patient Score weighs multiple factors that matter to you: clinical quality, response time, communication, appointment availability, and pricing transparency. We don't hide our methodology or sell placement to the highest bidder.

Real Patients, Real Data

Every score reflects actual patient experiences. We verify reviews to filter out spam and fake accounts. A provider with 500 verified patient ratings carries more weight than marketing claims on their own website.

Why This Matters

When you're choosing a telehealth provider, you need facts. Our scoring system cuts through the noise and gives you the information you actually need to make a choice.
