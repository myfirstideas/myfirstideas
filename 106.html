Varenicline is a prescription medication that helps people stop smoking by reducing cravings and easing withdrawal symptoms.

How varenicline works

Varenicline works by binding to nicotine receptors in your brain. This does two things at once: it blocks the rewarding effects of nicotine if you do smoke, and it reduces the urge to smoke in the first place.

How to take it

You'll typically start with a low dose and increase gradually over a week. Most people then take a standard dose for 11 weeks. The full course is 12 weeks total.

Side effects

Common side effects include nausea, sleep problems, and vivid dreams. Most people find nausea lessens after the first few days. Serious side effects are rare but possible; tell your doctor about mood changes or unusual thoughts.

Effectiveness

Varenicline roughly doubles your chances of quitting compared to trying without medication. Success rates improve further when combined with behavioural support.

Cost and access

Most insurance plans cover varenicline, though you may need prior authorisation. Generic versions are available and typically cost less than brand-name Chantix.

Talk to your doctor about whether varenicline is right for your situation.
