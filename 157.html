Who Can Get Phentermine

Phentermine is a controlled appetite suppressant prescribed for weight management. You typically qualify if you have a BMI of 30 or higher, or a BMI of 27 with weight-related health conditions like high blood pressure or diabetes.

Cost and Coverage

Phentermine itself is inexpensive, often 5 to 15 dollars per month without insurance. The real cost comes from the medical visit and ongoing monitoring. Telehealth visits range from 50 to 200 dollars per appointment, while in-person appointments may cost more. Most insurance plans do not cover phentermine, treating it as an elective medication.

Choosing a Prescriber

Look for prescribers who:

  • Require a medical history and current health assessment before writing a prescription
  • Offer follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and any side effects
  • Take time to discuss realistic weight loss expectations and lifestyle changes
  • Use telehealth or in-person care according to your needs
  • Are transparent about costs upfront

Avoid prescribers who skip medical evaluation, promise unrealistic results, or rush you through appointments.

What Happens Next

After you get a prescription, you will need to pick it up at a pharmacy. Costs vary by location and pharmacy; comparing prices can save you money. Your prescriber should schedule follow-up visits to check your progress and adjust your plan if needed.
