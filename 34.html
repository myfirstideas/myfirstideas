What boric acid does

Boric acid is a vaginal suppository inserted to treat yeast infections and bacterial vaginosis. It works by restoring vaginal pH balance and creating an environment where harmful bacteria and fungi struggle to survive.

How to use it

Boric acid suppositories are inserted vaginally, typically once daily for 5 to 14 days depending on the condition and your doctor's instructions. Most formulations contain 600 mg per suppository.

What to expect

You may experience mild vaginal irritation, discharge, or watery leakage. Avoid sexual contact during treatment. Results typically appear within a few days, though you should complete the full course even if symptoms improve.

Important cautions

Boric acid is for vaginal use only; it is toxic if swallowed. Do not use it if you are pregnant or if you have open vaginal wounds or severe irritation. If symptoms persist after treatment, see a doctor to rule out other infections or conditions.

Getting boric acid

Boric acid suppositories are available over the counter and do not require a prescription. You can purchase them at most pharmacies. Telehealth providers can also evaluate your symptoms and recommend or prescribe boric acid if appropriate.
