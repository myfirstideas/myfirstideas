What is zonisamide?

Zonisamide is an anticonvulsant medication prescribed to treat partial seizures in people with epilepsy. It works by reducing abnormal electrical activity in the brain that triggers seizures.

How zonisamide works

Zonisamide acts on sodium and calcium channels in nerve cells, stabilizing electrical activity and reducing the frequency of seizures. It may also have other mechanisms that contribute to its anticonvulsant effect.

Uses

Zonisamide is approved as an add-on treatment for partial seizures in adults and children. Doctors may prescribe it alongside other anticonvulsants when single-drug therapy is not effective.

Common side effects

People taking zonisamide commonly report:

  • Drowsiness and fatigue
  • Dizziness
  • Concentration problems
  • Nausea
  • Headache
  • Loss of appetite
  • Weight loss

Side effects often improve as your body adjusts to the medication.

Important considerations

Zonisamide can increase the risk of kidney stones, particularly in people with a personal or family history. Staying well hydrated may help reduce this risk. It can also cause a rash, which occasionally becomes serious. Tell your doctor about any skin changes immediately.

Do not stop taking zonisamide suddenly without medical guidance, as this can trigger seizures.

Cost and access

Zonisamide is available as a generic medication, making it more affordable than some brand-name anticonvulsants. Check your insurance coverage and compare pharmacy prices for the best rates.
