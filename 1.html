How we handle your data

We collect information to help you find the right telehealth provider and make informed decisions about your care.

What we collect

When you use RatedByPatients, we gather:

  • Information you give us directly (search queries, reviews, profile data)
  • Usage data from your browser and device
  • Location information (if you allow it)

We don't sell your personal information. We use it to improve our site, show you relevant providers, and fight fraud.

Your rights

You can:

  • Request what data we hold about you
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Download your data

How we protect it

We use encryption and security measures to keep your information safe. Our team has limited access, and we work with vendors who meet our privacy standards.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Reach out using the contact details on our privacy policy page.
