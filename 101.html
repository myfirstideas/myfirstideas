What Trimix is

Trimix is a compounded injectable medication for erectile dysfunction. It combines three active ingredients: alprostadil, papaverine, and phentolamine. Each ingredient works through a different mechanism to relax blood vessels and increase blood flow to the penis.

How you use it

Trimix is self-injected directly into the corpus cavernosum, the erectile tissue of the penis, using a very fine needle. Most men find the injection less intimidating in practice than in theory. You inject it 5 to 20 minutes before sexual activity.

What to expect

Erections typically develop within 5 to 20 minutes. The effect usually lasts 30 to 60 minutes, though this varies by dose and individual response. Because Trimix is compounded (mixed by a pharmacy rather than mass-manufactured), your doctor can adjust the concentrations of each ingredient to dial in your ideal dose.

Who it suits

Trimix works well for men who haven't responded to oral medications like sildenafil (Viagra) or tadalafil (Cialis), or who can't take them due to contraindications. It's also an option for men with diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or nerve damage affecting erectile function.

Cost and access

Trimix costs between 30 and 60 dollars per dose depending on the pharmacy and concentration. Since it's compounded, insurance rarely covers it, though some plans do. You'll need a prescription from a doctor or through a telemedicine provider who specialises in sexual health.
