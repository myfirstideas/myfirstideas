Getting Zepbound: Your Coverage and Cost Options

Zepbound (tirzepatide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist used for weight management. Accessing it depends on your insurance coverage, out-of-pocket budget, and medical eligibility.

Insurance Coverage Paths

Most commercial insurers now cover Zepbound, but coverage rules vary by plan:

  • Prior authorisation requirements: many plans require your doctor to justify medical necessity before approval
  • Step therapy: some plans require you to try and fail cheaper alternatives first
  • Quantity limits: coverage may cap doses per month or year
  • Formulary status: confirm Zepbound is on your plan's drug list, not a non-covered specialty tier

Call your insurer or check your plan documents to find your specific requirements.

Self-Pay Routes

If insurance won't cover Zepbound or you're uninsured:

  • Direct from providers: telehealth and weight loss clinics often sell Zepbound directly at per-dose pricing (typically GBP 200 to GBP 400 per injection)
  • Manufacturer savings programs: Eli Lilly offers patient assistance and copay cards that can reduce your cost significantly
  • Compounded versions: some pharmacies offer compounded tirzepatide at lower cost, though quality and consistency vary

What to Verify Before You Start

  1. Medical eligibility: confirm you meet BMI, weight loss plateau, or health condition thresholds
  2. Contraindications: check for personal or family history of thyroid cancer or multiple endocrine neoplasia
  3. Your actual out-of-pocket cost: factor in all doses needed for your treatment plan
  4. Pharmacy network: if insured, verify your pharmacy accepts your plan
  5. Follow-up care: confirm your provider will monitor you for side effects and efficacy
