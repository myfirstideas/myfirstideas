Mounjaro Price: What It Costs With and Without Insurance

Monjaro (tirzepatide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist used to treat type 2 diabetes. If you're paying out of pocket or managing insurance coverage, knowing the actual cost helps you plan treatment.

Mounjaro Cost Without Insurance

Mounjaro costs $1,000 to $1,300 per month without insurance. The exact price depends on:

  • Your pharmacy
  • Dosage strength (0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2.5 mg, 5 mg)
  • Quantity ordered
  • Geographic location

Monthly injections add up to $12,000 to $15,600 per year without coverage.

Mounjaro Cost With Insurance

Insurance copays typically range from $0 to $250 per month, depending on:

  • Your plan tier for GLP-1 medications
  • Deductible status
  • Whether your plan covers tirzepatide
  • Prior authorization requirements

Many commercial plans cover Mounjaro after prior authorization. Medicare coverage is limited; ask your plan administrator about your specific benefits.

Ways to Lower Mounjaro Costs

Manufacturer Coupon: Eli Lilly offers a patient savings program reducing out-of-pocket costs to $25 per month for eligible patients.

Patient Assistance Programs: Uninsured or underinsured patients may qualify for free medication through Eli Lilly's patient assistance.

GoodRx and Similar Services: These discount programs may offer lower prices than your insurance copay at certain pharmacies.

Generic Alternatives: Tirzepatide generics aren't available yet, but generic versions may launch in coming years.

Next Steps

Ask your doctor about prior authorization requirements, check whether Mounjaro appears on your plan's formulary, and explore manufacturer savings programs before filling your first prescription.
