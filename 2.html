What Calibrate does

Calibrate is a telehealth weight loss service that prescribes GLP-1 receptor agonist medications. The company pairs medication with coaching to help you manage weight.

How it works

You complete an online health assessment. A clinician reviews it and decides if you're a candidate for GLP-1 medication like semaglutide or tirzepatide. If approved, Calibrate ships medication to your home. You get ongoing support from health coaches and can check in with clinicians as needed.

Who it's for

Calibrate works with adults who have a BMI of 27 or higher, or a BMI of 25 or higher with weight-related health conditions. You'll need to live in a state where Calibrate operates and be willing to stick with the medication and coaching program.

Cost

Prices vary based on which medication you're prescribed and your location. Most plans start around 50 to 100 per month for the medication itself, plus coaching. Some insurance plans cover GLP-1 medications for weight loss; Calibrate's team can check your coverage.

What to know

GLP-1 medications work by slowing digestion and affecting appetite signals in your brain. Common side effects include nausea and constipation, especially when starting. Results take time: meaningful weight loss typically appears over months, not weeks. You'll need to stay on the medication to keep the weight off.
