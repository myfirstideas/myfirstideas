How to Get ED Medication Online

Getting ED medication online involves working with a licensed provider, usually through a telehealth platform. Here's what the process looks like.

Find a Telehealth Provider

Search for telehealth services that offer ED treatment. Many specialize in men's health and can connect you with a clinician in your state within hours.

Complete a Health Assessment

You'll answer questions about your medical history, current medications, and symptoms. This helps the provider determine if ED medication is safe for you.

Consult with a Clinician

A licensed doctor or nurse practitioner reviews your information via video, phone, or message. They'll discuss your options and any underlying causes.

Receive a Prescription

If approved, the clinician sends your prescription to a pharmacy. Many telehealth platforms partner with pharmacies for same-day or next-day delivery.

Understand the Costs

Telehealth visits typically cost 50 to 150 dollars. Medication prices vary by type and quantity. Some insurance plans cover ED treatment; others require out-of-pocket payment.

Compare Your Options

Common ED medications include sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, and avanafil. Prices and effectiveness vary. Ask your clinician which option fits your needs and budget.
