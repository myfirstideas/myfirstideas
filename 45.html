What dutasteride does

Dutasteride is a 5-alpha reductase inhibitor that stops your body converting testosterone into DHT, the hormone that shrinks hair follicles in male pattern baldness and enlarges the prostate in BPH.

Two FDA-approved uses

Dutasteride treats benign prostatic hyperplasia (enlarged prostate) under the brand name Avodart. Off-label, it's prescribed for androgenetic alopecia (male pattern baldness), where it's sold as Avodart or the generic.

How it compares to finasteride

Finasteride (Propecia, Proscar) blocks only one type of 5-alpha reductase. Dutasteride blocks both types, which means it lowers DHT more completely. Hair regrowth studies show dutasteride works faster and produces better results, but finasteride has a longer track record and lower cost.

Side effects

Sexual dysfunction is the most common serious side effect, affecting around 5% of users. Other reported effects include breast enlargement and reduced ejaculate volume. Most side effects occur in the first few months.

Cost and access

Dutasteride costs less as a generic than brand-name Avodart. Telehealth prescribers often charge $30 to $50 per month for the medication plus consultation fees. Insurance covers it for BPH but rarely for hair loss.

Timeline

Expect 3 to 6 months before you notice hair regrowth. Effects plateau around month 12.
