What is Semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, a class of medication that mimics glucagon-like peptide-1, a hormone that regulates appetite and blood sugar. It's prescribed for two main purposes: managing type 2 diabetes and supporting weight loss in people with obesity.

How Semaglutide Works

The medication works by:

  • Slowing stomach emptying to keep you feeling full longer
  • Reducing appetite signals to the brain
  • Improving how your body uses insulin
  • Helping regulate blood sugar levels

Who Takes Semaglutide

Semaglutide is prescribed for:

  • People with type 2 diabetes who need better blood sugar control
  • Adults with obesity (BMI 30 or higher) or overweight adults (BMI 27 or higher) with weight-related health conditions

Delivery and Dosing

Semaglutide comes as a once-weekly injection you give yourself under the skin. Doses start low and increase gradually over several weeks to minimize side effects.

Side Effects to Know About

Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation. These often improve as your body adjusts. More serious but rare side effects can include pancreatitis and thyroid concerns.

Getting Semaglutide Online

Many telehealth providers now prescribe semaglutide after an online consultation. You'll typically need a video visit with a clinician who can assess whether it's appropriate for you, then medication can be shipped to your home.

Cost Considerations

Semaglutide is expensive: brand-name Ozempic (for diabetes) and Wegovy (for weight loss) can cost $900 to $1,300 per month without insurance. Generic versions may offer some savings. Insurance coverage varies significantly by plan and indication.
