What Wegovy Costs

Wegovy (semaglutide) pricing splits clearly between insured and uninsured patients.

Without Insurance

Out-of-pocket Wegovy costs $1,500 to $1,800 per month. Novo Nordisk, the manufacturer, prices the full supply of weekly injections at this range. Some discount programs and copay assistance can reduce this to $500 to $900 monthly, but most patients without coverage face the higher price.

With Insurance

Insured patients typically pay $250 to $550 per month in copays or coinsurance. Your actual cost depends on:

  • Your plan's formulary (which drugs it covers)
  • Whether Wegovy requires prior authorization
  • Your deductible and coinsurance percentage
  • Your plan tier for this medication

Why Prices Differ

Insurers negotiate rebates and volume discounts with manufacturers that uninsured patients cannot access. These negotiations can reduce the net cost to insurers by 30 to 50 percent. That savings rarely flows to uninsured patients paying the full list price.

Coverage also varies by insurer. Some plans cover Wegovy only for patients with type 2 diabetes or a documented BMI threshold. Others require step therapy, meaning you must try cheaper medications first.

Getting Better Prices

If uninsured, ask about:

  • Novo Nordisk's Wegovy Savings Card (can reduce cost to around $250 per month)
  • GoodRx or similar prescription discount programs
  • Telehealth services that offer lower-cost compounded semaglutide alternatives

If insured, contact your plan before starting Wegovy to confirm coverage and any prior authorization requirements.
