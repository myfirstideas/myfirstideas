Ozempic Cost: What You Actually Pay With and Without Insurance

Ozempic (semaglutide) is expensive. Here's what the actual numbers look like.

Without Insurance

The list price for Ozempic runs between $800 and $1,500 per month, depending on your dose. Most people don't pay this. Manufacturer coupons and patient assistance programs can drop your cost to $25 to $250 per month if you qualify.

With Insurance

Insurance coverage varies widely. Your copay typically ranges from $50 to $500 per month. Some plans cover it fully for type 2 diabetes but not for weight loss. Others require prior authorization or step therapy, meaning you must try and fail cheaper medications first.

Ways to Lower Your Cost

  • Check Novo Nordisk's patient assistance program (the manufacturer)
  • Use GoodRx or similar discount cards, which sometimes undercut insurance copays
  • Ask about generic semaglutide if your doctor approves
  • Compare mail order vs. retail pharmacy pricing
  • Verify whether your plan covers the indication your doctor is prescribing for

Call your insurance company directly. Automated sites often give outdated information, and representatives can tell you your exact copay and any restrictions that apply to you specifically.
