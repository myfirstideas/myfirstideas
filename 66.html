What Metronidazole Does

Metronidazole is an antibiotic that kills bacteria and parasites causing infections. Doctors prescribe it for:

  • Bacterial vaginosis
  • Trichomoniasis
  • Certain intestinal parasites
  • Dental infections
  • Skin infections from anaerobic bacteria

How to Take It

Metronidazole comes as tablets, capsules, or liquid. Take it exactly as prescribed, usually with food to reduce stomach upset. Complete the full course even if you feel better.

Common Side Effects

Most people tolerate metronidazole well. Watch for:

  • Metallic taste in mouth
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Headache
  • Dark urine (normal)
  • Diarrhea

Serious side effects are rare but report nerve pain, confusion, or severe allergic reactions to your doctor immediately.

Cost and Insurance

Metronidazole is generic and inexpensive, typically 10 to 30 dollars for a standard course without insurance. Most plans cover it.

Important Drug Interactions

Metronidazole interacts with alcohol, causing severe nausea and vomiting. Avoid alcohol during treatment and for 48 hours after your last dose. Tell your doctor about all medications, especially blood thinners and lithium.

Before You Start

Disclose to your doctor if you have:

  • Liver disease
  • Nervous system disorders
  • Blood disorders
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding plans

Metronidazole is generally safe in pregnancy after the first trimester for serious infections, but your doctor will weigh risks and benefits.
