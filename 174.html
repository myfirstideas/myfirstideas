The Wegovy savings card sounds straightforward: it caps your monthly out-of-pocket costs at $250. But that number only applies if you have commercial insurance and meet the card's eligibility requirements.

How the Wegovy savings card works

Novo Nordisk's savings card reduces your copay, coinsurance or out-of-pocket maximum to a set amount per month. For most people with commercial insurance, that amount is $250. The card covers the difference between what you pay and what Novo Nordisk charges the insurer.

When the card helps

The savings card kicks in when:

  • You have commercial health insurance (not Medicare, Medicaid or VA coverage)
  • Your insurance plan covers Wegovy
  • Your out-of-pocket costs would otherwise be higher than the card's limit

If your copay is already $25 a month, the card doesn't lower it further. If your coinsurance works out to $400 a month, the card brings it down to $250.

What the card won't do

The savings card does not apply if you're uninsured, on Medicare, on Medicaid or covered by the VA. If you fall into one of these groups, Novo Nordisk offers assistance programs with different eligibility rules and benefit levels.

Real costs vary by plan

Your actual monthly cost depends on your specific insurance plan's coverage, deductible status and how your plan categorizes Wegovy. The savings card sets a ceiling, not a floor.
