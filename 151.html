Finding Affordable Tirzepatide: Your Cost Comparison Guide

Tirzepatide costs vary wildly depending where you buy it. A month's supply can range from under $100 to over $1,000 at different pharmacies and telehealth clinics.

Why prices differ so much

Tirzepatide isn't yet available as a generic, so every supplier sets their own markup. Telehealth providers stack consultation fees on top of medication costs. Mail-order pharmacies compete on price in ways local chains sometimes don't.

How to find lower prices

Compare across telehealth platforms. The same dose costs different amounts at different providers. Get quotes before committing to a prescription.

Check major pharmacy chains. Walgreens, CVS and Walmart prices can undercut specialty pharmacies, especially if you have insurance.

Look for manufacturer assistance. Eli Lilly's patient assistance program can reduce or cover costs if you qualify by income.

Watch for hidden fees. Some telehealth providers advertise a low medication price but add consultation, shipping or membership costs that inflate the total.

Red flags for overpriced suppliers

Any provider claiming their tirzepatide is "pharmaceutical grade" or "clinical quality" above others is using marketing rather than facts. Tirzepatide is tirzepatide. Extreme markups (often over 400%) signal profit-first operations rather than cost-focused ones.

What you actually need to compare

Don't just look at per-dose cost. Calculate the full monthly or quarterly expense including all fees. Factor in whether you need a follow-up consultation in your timeframe. Insurance coverage can shift which option saves the most money.
