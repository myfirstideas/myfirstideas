Zepbound vs Wegovy: The Same Drug, Different Prices

Zepbound and Wegovy both contain semaglutide. The difference is what the FDA approved them for, and how much you pay.

The Active Ingredient is Identical

Both drugs are semaglutide. Both work the same way in your body. Both come in the same doses. The difference is the indication on the label.

Zepbound is approved for weight management in adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related conditions. Wegovy is approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related conditions. Same indication, different brand.

Actually: Zepbound is for weight management. Wegovy is for weight loss. Both are used off-label for the opposite indication.

Results

Clinical trials show weight loss of 15% to 22% of starting body weight over 68 weeks with semaglutide. Real world results vary. Some people lose more. Some lose less. Some stop because of side effects.

Side Effects

Common side effects: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain. Most happen early and fade. Serious side effects are rare but documented: pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, changes in vision, kidney problems, thoughts of self-harm.

Cost

Zepbound list price: around 1,350 dollars per month. Wegovy list price: around 1,350 dollars per month. Actual out-of-pocket cost depends on your insurance, your copay, whether you qualify for a discount program.

Many insurance plans do not cover either drug for weight management. Some cover Wegovy for diabetes. Discount programs can cut the price to 250 dollars or less per month.

The Bottom Line

They are the same drug. Pick the one your insurance covers. If your insurance covers neither, negotiate the price or look for a telehealth provider with a discount program.
