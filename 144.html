What are GLP-1 drugs?

GLP-1 receptor agonists are medications that regulate blood sugar and appetite. They're prescribed for type 2 diabetes and, increasingly, for weight management.

Common GLP-1 medication names

The main options available in the US market are:

  • Semaglutide (Ozempic for diabetes, Wegovy for weight loss)
  • Tirzepatide (Zepbound for weight loss, Mounjaro for diabetes)
  • Dulaglutide (Trulicity)
  • Liraglutide (Saxenda for weight loss, Victoza for diabetes)

Cost breakdown

GLP-1 drugs range from $900 to $1,500 per month without insurance. Brand-name versions cost significantly more than generics, though generics remain limited in this class.

Insurance coverage varies. Most plans cover these drugs for diabetes. Weight loss coverage is inconsistent and often requires prior authorization or proof of medical necessity.

Common side effects

Expect gastrointestinal effects, especially early on:

  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Constipation or diarrhea
  • Stomach pain
  • Loss of appetite

Side effects typically improve within weeks but can persist. Rare serious side effects include pancreatitis and thyroid concerns.

How to access GLP-1 medications

  1. Get a prescription. Schedule with your primary care doctor or a specialist (endocrinologist, weight loss specialist).
  2. Check insurance coverage. Call your plan or use the provider's patient portal.
  3. Explore patient assistance programs. Manufacturers offer copay cards and free medication for uninsured patients.
  4. Consider telehealth. Many telemedicine platforms now prescribe GLP-1 drugs, often with lower consultation fees than in-person visits.
  5. Compare pharmacies. Prices vary significantly between retail, mail order, and discount programs like GoodRx.

Bottom line

GLP-1 drugs are effective but expensive. Insurance, patient assistance programs, and telehealth options can reduce costs. Start with your doctor to understand your coverage and eligibility.
