What is RatedByPatients Plus?

RatedByPatients Plus is a membership option that removes ads from the site and gives you priority customer support when you need help comparing telehealth and telemedicine providers.

Why choose Plus?

If you use the site regularly to research providers, find ratings, or compare telehealth options, the ad-free experience pays for itself in cleaner browsing. You get faster responses when you contact support.

How it works

Sign up for Plus and your account switches immediately. No ads load on any page. Support requests get prioritised in our queue.

Who should get it?

Plus works best for people who visit the site multiple times a week or who are doing serious research into provider options and want nothing in the way of the information.
