What is enclomiphene?

Enclomiphene is the active isomer of clomiphene citrate. It's the part of the drug that actually does the work in your body.

How it works

Enclomiphene blocks estrogen receptors in the brain, which triggers your pituitary gland to release more follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH). This signals your testicles to produce more testosterone.

It's used off-label in men with low testosterone who want to preserve fertility, because it stimulates natural testosterone production instead of replacing it.

What it treats

Enclomiphene is prescribed off-label for:

  • Low testosterone (hypogonadism) in men
  • Male infertility related to low testosterone
  • Testosterone recovery after anabolic steroid use

Side effects

Common side effects include:

  • Blurred vision
  • Headaches
  • Nausea
  • Mood changes
  • Breast tenderness

Visual side effects are more common with clomiphene citrate (the full drug) than with enclomiphene alone.

Important: not FDA-approved

Enclomiphene as a standalone drug is not FDA-approved in the US. It's available through compounding pharmacies and telemedicine providers, often prescribed off-label.

Cost and access

Cost varies by provider and prescription strength. Telemedicine clinics typically charge for consultations and medication separately.
