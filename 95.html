Testosterone undecanoate is a synthetic androgen used to treat hypogonadism, a condition where the body produces too little testosterone.

How it works

Testosterone undecanoate is a modified form of testosterone designed to stay active in your body longer than standard testosterone. It's given as an injection, typically every 10 to 14 weeks.

Who takes it

Doctors prescribe testosterone undecanoate to people with clinically low testosterone levels caused by conditions affecting the testes, pituitary gland or hypothalamus.

What to expect

Most people need several months to feel the full effects. The drug is given as an intramuscular injection, usually into the glute or thigh.

Side effects

Common side effects include acne, increased appetite and mood changes. More serious risks include changes to cholesterol levels and potential effects on the heart or prostate. You'll need regular blood tests to monitor testosterone levels and other markers while taking this medication.

Cost and access

Price varies widely depending on your insurance coverage and pharmacy. GoodRx and similar discount programs sometimes offer lower costs than your regular copay.
