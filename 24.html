How We Make Money: Our Affiliate Disclosure

We get asked often how we stay independent while reviewing telehealth and telemedicine services. The answer is straightforward: affiliate commissions.

What This Means

When you click a link to a telehealth provider, pharmacy, or insurance plan from our site and sign up or make a purchase, we may earn a commission. You don't pay extra. The price you see is the price you pay.

Why We Disclose This

We believe you deserve to know how we're funded. No surprises, no hidden relationships, no conflicts buried in legal footnotes.

How It Affects Our Reviews

Our affiliate relationships don't change our editorial standards. We review telehealth services, medications, and insurance plans based on:

  • What real patients report
  • Actual costs and coverage
  • How providers compare on service, speed, and reliability
  • Data over marketing claims

We won't recommend something just because it pays commission. We also won't exclude something that works well because it doesn't.

Your Trust Matters

If you spot a review that feels off or a comparison that doesn't add up, tell us. Our credibility depends on being honest about what we know, what we don't know, and how we stay in business.

Questions?

Contact us directly if you want to know more about how we work or why we've made a particular recommendation.
