Telehealth Costs: What You'll Actually Pay

Telehealth visit costs vary sharply depending on your insurance, the provider, and what you need.

Common Price Points

Uninsured visits typically run 40 to 100 dollars per consultation. Some platforms charge flat rates; others adjust by specialist type.

With insurance, your cost depends on your plan's copay structure. Many plans treat telehealth the same as in-person visits. That means a 20 to 40 dollar copay, or coinsurance of 10 to 20 percent of the visit fee.

What Affects Your Final Bill

Provider type matters. A nurse consultation costs less than seeing a board-certified physician. Therapy sessions run differently than acute care visits.

Timing factors in too. Evening or weekend appointments sometimes carry premium pricing.

Your deductible status changes what you owe. If you haven't met your deductible, you pay more upfront. If you have, you drop to your copay.

Ways to Lower Your Cost

Check your plan's formulary and in-network provider list before booking. Some insurers cover telehealth at zero cost for preventive care.

Community health centers and urgent care telehealth options often undercut standalone telehealth platforms.

Ask about transparent pricing upfront. Reputable platforms quote costs before you commit to a visit.
