What is Guanfacine?

Guanfacine is a non-stimulant medication that works by relaxing blood vessels and reducing nerve activity in the brain. It's prescribed for two main conditions: attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and high blood pressure.

How Guanfacine Works

Guanfacine is an alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. It stimulates receptors in the brain that help improve focus and reduce impulsive behaviour in people with ADHD. For high blood pressure, it works by widening blood vessels, which lowers pressure on artery walls.

Uses

  • ADHD in children and adults
  • High blood pressure
  • Sometimes prescribed off-label for anxiety or sleep problems

Common Side Effects

Most people experience mild side effects that often improve over time:

  • Drowsiness or fatigue
  • Dizziness
  • Dry mouth
  • Headache
  • Nausea

Important Considerations

Guanfacine works best when taken regularly at the same time each day. Do not stop taking it suddenly, as this can cause a rapid increase in blood pressure. Always follow your doctor's instructions on dosing and changes to your treatment plan.
