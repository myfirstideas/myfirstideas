What tadalafil is

Tadalafil is a phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor, a class of medication that relaxes smooth muscle and increases blood flow. It's used to treat erectile dysfunction and benign prostatic hyperplasia (enlarged prostate).

How it works

Tadalafil works by blocking phosphodiesterase-5, an enzyme that breaks down cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP). This allows cGMP to accumulate, which relaxes smooth muscle in the corpus cavernosum of the penis and increases blood flow.

Dosing

For erectile dysfunction, tadalafil comes in 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, and 20 mg tablets. A doctor will determine the right dose for you based on your health and other medications.

For benign prostatic hyperplasia, the typical dose is 5 mg once daily.

Cost

Tadalafil is available as a generic medication, which costs significantly less than brand-name Cialis. Generic tadalafil typically costs between 0.50 and 2 dollars per dose depending on the dose strength and where you fill the prescription.

Side effects

Common side effects include headache, indigestion, back pain, muscle aches, flushing, and nasal congestion. Serious side effects are rare but can include sudden vision or hearing loss, chest pain, and priapism (prolonged erection).
