Hydroxyzine is an antihistamine that doctors prescribe for anxiety, itching, and sleep problems. It is not a benzodiazepine or an SSRI, which means it works differently and carries a lower risk of dependence.

How hydroxyzine works

Hydroxyzine blocks histamine receptors in your brain and central nervous system. This reduces anxiety signals and promotes drowsiness. Most people feel the effects within 15 to 30 minutes of taking it.

Cost and availability

A month's supply of hydroxyzine typically costs around 10 dollars with a generic prescription. Brand names include Atarax and Vistaril, which cost more.

Common uses

Doctors prescribe hydroxyzine for:

  • Generalized anxiety disorder
  • Pre-procedure anxiety
  • Itching from allergies or skin conditions
  • Insomnia
  • Nausea

Side effects and considerations

Common side effects include drowsiness, dry mouth, and blurred vision. Hydroxyzine can interact with alcohol and other central nervous system depressants. Tell your doctor about all medications you take before starting hydroxyzine.
