Telehealth in the US works in straightforward steps.

Find a provider

You pick a telehealth service or your existing doctor's video appointment option. Some services let you book same-day or next-day appointments.

Schedule and prepare

Book your appointment through an app or website. You'll need a device with a camera and microphone, plus a reliable internet connection. Log in a few minutes early.

Meet your doctor

You'll video call with a licensed doctor or nurse practitioner in your state. They'll take your medical history, listen to your symptoms, and examine you using video.

Get treatment

If you need medication, your doctor can send a prescription to a pharmacy of your choice. For other conditions, they may refer you to in-person care or recommend follow-up telehealth visits.

Pay for it

Costs vary: uninsured visits typically run 40 to 200 dollars. Insurance usually covers telehealth at the same rate as in-person visits if the provider is in your network. Check your plan before booking.
