Telehealth and Telemedicine Reviews, Ratings and Comparisons

Choosing a telehealth or telemedicine provider means weighing real factors: cost, availability, which conditions they handle, whether they accept your insurance, and what past patients actually experienced.

RatedByPatients cuts through the noise. We publish specific numbers, patient ratings, and straightforward comparisons so you can make a decision based on fact rather than marketing claims.

What We Cover

We review and compare telehealth and telemedicine platforms, individual clinicians, specific medications and treatments, insurance coverage for virtual care, and conditions commonly managed online.

Our ratings reflect what patients report: how easy a service was to use, whether the clinician listened, if pricing matched what was quoted, and whether virtual care actually solved the problem.

How to Use This Site

Start by searching for a specific provider, platform, condition, or medication. Read patient reviews and ratings alongside the facts: accepted insurance plans, appointment costs, typical wait times, and what services are actually available in your state.

Compare options side by side. Check whether a provider is licensed in your location and whether they handle your condition. Look at the numbers before the testimonials.

Telehealth and telemedicine work best when you know what you are getting into before you book.
