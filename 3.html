What Found Offers

Found is a telehealth platform focused on weight loss and metabolic health. The service connects patients with clinicians who prescribe medications like GLP-1 agonists, along with nutrition guidance and ongoing monitoring.

How It Works

Patients complete an online health assessment, consult with a clinician via telehealth, and receive a treatment plan that typically includes medication and behavioral support. Refills and follow-up consultations happen through the platform.

Cost and Insurance

Found charges a monthly membership fee plus the cost of medications. Many insurance plans cover the prescription medications if clinically appropriate, though coverage varies. The company also offers direct pay options.

Medications Available

Found prescribes evidence-based weight loss medications including GLP-1 agonists and other FDA-approved options. Medication selection depends on individual health history and clinical assessment.

Clinical Support

Treatment plans include regular check-ins with clinicians, nutrition guidance, and medication adjustments based on progress and side effects.

When to Consider Found

Found works well for people seeking structured weight loss treatment with medication support, ongoing clinical oversight, and behavioral coaching through a single integrated platform.
