What hydroquinone does

Hydroquinone is a topical medication that lightens areas of hyperpigmentation on the skin. It works by reducing the production of melanin, the pigment that gives skin its colour.

Common uses include treating:

  • Age spots and sun spots
  • Melasma (brown patches on the face)
  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (dark marks left after acne or injury)
  • Freckles

How to use it

Hydroquinone comes as a cream, gel, or lotion. Standard strengths are 2%, 4%, and prescription-only 4% formulations. Most people apply it once or twice daily to clean, dry skin.

Results typically take 4 to 6 weeks to appear, with full effects within 8 to 12 weeks.

Side effects

Common side effects include:

  • Skin irritation or redness
  • Dryness
  • Itching
  • Temporary darkening of the skin (paradoxical darkening)

More serious but rare side effects include ochronosis, a condition where the skin becomes dark and thick with long-term use.

Getting hydroquinone online

Telehealth providers can prescribe hydroquinone, though availability varies. Some online dermatology services offer consultations for pigmentation issues and may issue prescriptions if appropriate.

OTC hydroquinone (2% strength) is available without a prescription. Prescription-strength formulations (4%) require a doctor's assessment.
