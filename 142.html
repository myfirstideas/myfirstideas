Mounjaro $25 Savings Card: What You Actually Pay

Lily's copay assistance program caps your monthly out-of-pocket cost at $25 for Mounjaro (tirzepatide). But the card doesn't work the same way for everyone.

How the $25 Cap Works

The savings card reduces your copay to $25 per month if you have commercial insurance. Lily covers the difference between your copay and the actual drug cost. The card is free to use and doesn't require a deductible to be met first.

Who Qualifies

You need:

  • Commercial health insurance (not Medicare or Medicaid)
  • A valid Mounjaro prescription
  • To be a US resident

If you're uninsured or underinsured, Lily offers a separate patient assistance program with different eligibility rules.

What Happens If Your Coverage Changes

You'll need to enrol in a new savings card if you switch insurance plans or lose coverage. The program doesn't transfer automatically between plans. Enrol early to avoid gaps in assistance.

Cost Without Insurance

Mounjaro costs around $1,100 per month at full price. Without the savings card or patient assistance, this is what you'd pay out of pocket.

Next Steps

Ask your doctor about the savings card at your next appointment, or enrol directly through Lily's website before filling your prescription.
