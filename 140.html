Ozempic Copay Card: Coverage Details and Eligibility

Novo Nordisk's copay card for Ozempic can cut your out-of-pocket costs significantly, but eligibility rules are strict and the program has real limits.

How Much the Card Pays

The Ozempic copay card caps your copay at $250 per monthly prescription for most plans. The manufacturer covers the difference between your standard copay and $250.

You must have commercial (private) insurance to use it. Medicare and Medicaid patients cannot use this card.

Who Qualifies

You need:

  • Commercial health insurance with Ozempic coverage
  • A prescription from your doctor
  • No prior authorization denials on file

Some plans exclude the card. Check with your insurer first.

What Happens If You Don't Qualify

If you have Medicare or Medicaid, or if your plan doesn't accept the card:

  • Ask your doctor about patient assistance programs run by Novo Nordisk
  • Request generic alternatives like semaglutide if available on your plan
  • Explore whether GLP-1 drugs covered by your insurance might work instead
  • Contact 211.org to find local pharmaceutical assistance resources

Key Limits

The card doesn't reduce your deductible. If you haven't met your deductible yet, you pay the full price before the copay cap kicks in.

It also won't cover Ozempic if your insurer denies the prescription outright.

Next Steps

Call your insurer to confirm Ozempic is covered and that the copay card works with your specific plan. Get clarity on deductible status before filling your first prescription.
