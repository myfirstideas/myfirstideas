Zepbound is a prescription medication for weight management containing semaglutide. It's designed for adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related health conditions.

How Zepbound Works

Zepbound works by mimicking glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), a hormone that regulates hunger and blood sugar. This mechanism helps reduce appetite and increase feelings of fullness.

Typical Dosing

Zepbound comes as a once-weekly injection. Treatment starts with a low dose and increases gradually over several weeks to reduce side effects and allow your body to adjust.

Common Side Effects

Most people experience mild to moderate side effects, particularly when starting or increasing the dose. These typically include nausea, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, and fatigue. Side effects usually improve over time as your body adjusts.

Cost and Insurance

Zepbound costs around 1,400 to 1,500 dollars per month without insurance. Many insurance plans cover it when medically necessary, though prior authorization may be required. Cost-sharing varies by plan.

Getting Zepbound

You'll need a prescription from a healthcare provider. Many people access it through telemedicine appointments, which can be faster and more convenient than in-person visits.
