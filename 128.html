Online UTI Treatment: How to Choose a Service Without Overpaying

Urinary tract infections are painful and common, but treating them online has become straightforward. The challenge is finding a service that charges fairly without inflating costs for basic care.

Why Price Varies So Widely

Online UTI treatment costs range from £30 to £150+ per consultation. This gap exists because:

  • Some services charge flat consultation fees regardless of complexity
  • Others layer on pharmacy markups that double antibiotic costs
  • Insurance coverage varies significantly by provider and plan
  • Virtual-only clinics often undercut hospital-affiliated services

What You Actually Pay For

Break down the cost:

  1. Consultation fee (£20 to £80): The video or chat appointment with a clinician
  2. Prescription cost (£10 to £60): The antibiotic itself, depending on type and quantity
  3. Pharmacy markup (varies): Some services profit heavily here
  4. Delivery fees (£0 to £10): Not all charge this; some include it

Red Flags That Signal Overpricing

  • Consultation fees above £80 for a straightforward UTI diagnosis
  • Antibiotics priced 40% above high street pharmacy rates
  • Mandatory add-ons like "premium consultations" for standard care
  • No price transparency before booking

How to Compare Fairly

Get the total price upfront: Ask for consultation plus medication cost before committing. Many services hide the full price until checkout.

Check against high street pharmacies: Use sites like GPC or Pharmacy2U to see what antibiotics cost separately, then compare total telehealth pricing.

Use your insurance if available: Some plans cover telehealth UTI consultations fully or at reduced copay. This can undercut out-of-pocket pricing significantly.

Verify clinician credentials: Cheaper isn't better if the service uses unqualified staff. Confirm doctors and nurses are registered with the GMC or NMC.

When to Choose Telemedicine Over Your GP

Telemedicine works best when you need speed and have a straightforward infection. Same-day or next-day appointments are standard. Your GP might have a 2 to 3 week wait.

Choose your GP if: you have recurrent UTIs, you're pregnant, or you need tests beyond urinalysis.

The Bottom Line

Online UTI treatment at £40 to £70 total (consultation plus antibiotic) is fair pricing. Anything above £100 suggests either premium branding or hidden markups. Compare the full cost, check clinician credentials, and verify it's not cheaper to buy the antibiotic separately after a cheaper consultation elsewhere.
