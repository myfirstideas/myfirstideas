Amphetamine salts (Adderall): the essentials

Amphetamine salts, sold under the brand name Adderall, treat attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and narcolepsy. They're a controlled substance that works by increasing dopamine and norepinephrine in the brain.

How they work

Amphetamine salts stimulate the central nervous system. They improve focus, attention and impulse control by raising levels of neurotransmitters that regulate these functions.

Who takes them

Doctors prescribe amphetamine salts to:

  • Adults and children with ADHD
  • People with narcolepsy
  • Some patients with treatment-resistant depression (off-label)

Side effects

Common effects include insomnia, loss of appetite, increased heart rate and anxiety. Less common but serious effects include heart problems and psychiatric symptoms.

Cost and access

Amphetamine salts are available as immediate-release and extended-release formulations. Generic versions cost less than brand-name Adderall. Many insurance plans cover them, though prior authorisation is sometimes required.

You'll need a prescription from a doctor or prescriber. Some telehealth providers can evaluate you for ADHD and issue prescriptions.

Getting a prescription online

Telehealth ADHD services let you consult with a clinician from home. If they determine you're a candidate, they can send your prescription to a pharmacy. Not all providers prescribe controlled substances like amphetamine salts; check their policies first.
