How We Review Telehealth Providers and Keep Our Ratings Honest

At RatedByPatients, we publish reviews and comparisons of telehealth and telemedicine providers. Our readers depend on us to tell them which services actually work, which ones cost what they say they cost, and which ones have real limitations worth knowing about.

That means our editorial standards have to be specific enough to mean something.

What We Actually Do

We test telehealth services ourselves. We check how long it takes to see a clinician. We verify what the site says about credentials, licensing, and what medications they prescribe. We look at the fine print on costs, including hidden fees and insurance handling. We compare what people say they're getting against what they're actually paying.

We don't just collect other people's reviews and publish them. We do original reporting on how telehealth providers actually operate.

What We Won't Do

We don't accept payment from telehealth providers to review them. We don't let advertising relationships change how we rate services. We don't let investment stakes or commercial partnerships affect what we publish. No provider gets a favourable review because they spend money with us.

We disclose conflicts of interest clearly, every time. When a source has a stake in what we're saying, you see it.

We don't use unverified claims. We cite our sources. When we quote research, we link to it. When we report on a provider's practices, we explain where that information came from.

How We Write About Telehealth

We use numbers over adjectives. We tell you what something costs, how long you wait, what percentage of patients report each outcome. We avoid hype and we avoid fear-mongering. We assume you've read the fine print and you want the truth about what you're actually getting.

We're specific about limitations. Telehealth isn't right for every condition or every person. We explain which services work for what, and we explain the gaps. We don't pretend every telehealth option is a replacement for in-person care when it isn't.

Updates and Corrections

Telehealth services change. Pricing changes. Availability changes. Regulations change. We update our reviews when the facts change. We correct errors as soon as we find them, and we note what changed and when.

If you spot something that's wrong, tell us. Our contact information is on every page.
