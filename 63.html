What is lisdexamfetamine?

Lisdexamfetamine is a prescription stimulant medication used to treat attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in children and adults. It's a prodrug, meaning your body converts it to dexamphetamine after you take it.

Brand names include Vyvanse and generic versions are available.

How it works

Lisdexamfetamine increases dopamine and norepinephrine levels in your brain, which helps improve focus, attention and impulse control.

Dosing

Doses typically range from 20 mg to 70 mg once daily, taken in the morning. Your doctor will start low and increase gradually based on how you respond.

Side effects

Common side effects include:

  • Decreased appetite
  • Sleep problems
  • Increased heart rate
  • Headache
  • Dry mouth
  • Anxiety

Serious side effects are rare but can include heart problems or mental health changes. Tell your doctor about any concerning symptoms.

Cost and insurance

Cost varies significantly based on your insurance and whether you use a generic version. Many insurers require prior authorization. Generic lisdexamfetamine is typically cheaper than brand name Vyvanse.

Who it's for

Lisdexamfetamine is approved for ADHD in children 6 and older and adults. It's not suitable if you have:

  • Heart problems
  • High blood pressure
  • Hyperthyroidism
  • Glaucoma
  • A personal or family history of substance use disorder

Next steps

If you're considering lisdexamfetamine, talk to your doctor about whether it's the right choice for your situation, how it compares to other ADHD medications, and what to expect.
