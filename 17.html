What Matters in a Telehealth Comparison

Providers vary sharply on several fronts:

Cost structure: Some charge per visit, others use subscriptions. Some integrate with insurance; others don't. Out-of-pocket costs swing wildly depending on your plan and condition.

Speed: Wait times range from same-day appointments to scheduled visits weeks out. Urgent care telemedicine moves faster than therapy platforms.

Scope: One platform might handle primary care and prescriptions. Another focuses on mental health only. Check what conditions and specialties each one actually covers.

Coverage: Insurance acceptance, out-of-network policies and pharmacy partnerships differ. A platform that works smoothly with your insurer saves money and hassle.

Support: Response times, clinician availability and how platforms handle follow-ups matter when you need care, not just when you book it.
