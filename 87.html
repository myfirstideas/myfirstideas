Sildenafil is a phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor taken by mouth to treat erectile dysfunction and pulmonary hypertension. It works by relaxing blood vessel walls and increasing blood flow.

The medication takes effect in 30 to 60 minutes and lasts around 4 to 6 hours. Most men take one dose as needed before sexual activity, though dosing varies depending on your health and other medications.

Sildenafil comes in 25mg, 50mg and 100mg tablets. Generic sildenafil costs roughly 50 to 80 percent less than brand-name Viagra, making it a common first-line choice for cost-conscious patients.

Common side effects include headache, flushing, indigestion and nasal congestion. Serious side effects are rare but include sudden vision loss and priapism, a prolonged erection requiring emergency care.

You cannot take sildenafil with nitrate medications used for heart conditions. It also interacts with certain blood pressure drugs, alpha blockers and some protease inhibitors.

Telemedicine providers often prescribe sildenafil after an online consultation. Many telehealth services offer home delivery and discreet packaging. Prices vary widely depending on the provider, your insurance and whether you choose brand or generic versions.
