Telehealth Doctors, Reviewed

Finding the right telehealth doctor means checking the facts that matter: credentials, availability, cost and what actual patients say about the experience.

Our reviews cut through marketing speak. We look at board certification, response times, consultation fees and patient ratings across multiple telehealth platforms. You get numbers, not adjectives.

What to check when choosing a telehealth doctor

  • Board certification and medical licensing
  • Average consultation costs and what insurance covers
  • How long you typically wait for an appointment
  • Patient ratings focused on real outcomes
  • Whether they handle your specific condition or prescription need

Telehealth doctors work within the same regulatory frameworks as in-person physicians. Your state's medical board oversees their credentials. The difference is where the appointment happens.

Start by identifying what you need the doctor for. Some telehealth doctors specialise in acute care; others focus on ongoing management of chronic conditions. Some can prescribe; others offer diagnosis only. Pricing varies significantly between platforms and individual doctors.

Read patient reviews carefully. Look for specific details about communication, whether the doctor took time to listen, and whether the treatment worked. Dismiss reviews that praise or criticise the platform rather than the doctor's clinical skills.

Check whether the doctor is in-network with your insurance. Telehealth visits can be cheaper than office visits, but only if you're not paying out-of-pocket for an out-of-network provider.
