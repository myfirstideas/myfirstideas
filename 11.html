GLP-1 drugs cost wildly different amounts depending on where you fill them. A month's supply of Ozempic can run anywhere from $250 to $1,200 at different pharmacies, and telehealth providers price Wegovy differently again.

Why GLP-1 Prices Vary So Much

Manufacturer discounts, pharmacy markups, telehealth platform fees, and insurance coverage all shift the final number you pay. A generic version costs less than the brand name, but availability is still patchy in most states.

How to Reduce What You Pay

  • Check multiple telehealth providers; some bundle medication with consultation fees, others charge separately
  • Use GoodRx or similar discount cards at retail pharmacies if you're uninsured
  • Ask your insurance plan which GLP-1 drugs they cover and at what tier
  • Buy three-month supplies where possible; per-dose costs drop
  • Look for manufacturer coupons; Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly both offer savings programs
