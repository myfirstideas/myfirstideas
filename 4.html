Hers Weight Loss Overview

Hers is a telehealth platform that prescribes weight loss medications online. The service connects you with clinicians who assess your health history and prescribe GLP-1 receptor agonists like semaglutide and tirzepatide, along with lifestyle guidance.

How It Works

You complete a health questionnaire and have a video or messaging consultation with a clinician. If approved, prescriptions go directly to a pharmacy. Follow-up care happens through the app with ongoing clinician support.

Cost

Initial consultations typically run 50 to 100 dollars. Medication costs depend on the specific prescription, supplier, and your insurance coverage. Many people pay out-of-pocket, though some insurance plans cover weight loss medications.

Medications Available

Hers prescribes GLP-1 medications including semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Zepbound). Your clinician determines which medication suits your health profile and weight loss goals.

Who It's For

Hers weight loss services suit people with a BMI over 27 (or 25 with weight-related conditions) who want prescription medication support for weight management. The platform is not suitable if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a personal or family history of thyroid cancer.

Considerations

GLP-1 medications can cause nausea, vomiting, or constipation. Insurance coverage varies widely. Some people experience costs that reach hundreds of dollars monthly without coverage.
