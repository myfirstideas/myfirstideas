RatedByPatients is a comparison site built for people who want straight answers about telehealth and telemedicine options.

We cut through the marketing noise and focus on what matters: real information about providers, insurers, pharmacies, conditions and medications. No hype, no fear, just numbers and specifics you can rely on.

Our approach is plain-spoken and money-literate. We read the fine print so you don't have to. Every comparison, guide and review reflects the actual search patterns and questions people bring to telehealth and telemedicine, not what we think they should care about.

Whether you're exploring virtual care options, comparing plans, or trying to understand a medication or condition, RatedByPatients gives you the clarity to choose.
