What is naltrexone-bupropion

Naltrexone-bupropion is a combination medication sold as Contrave. It pairs two active ingredients: naltrexone, an opioid antagonist, and bupropion, an antidepressant that also affects appetite. The combination is designed for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight.

How it works

Bupropion acts on areas of the brain that control hunger and energy expenditure. Naltrexone enhances bupropion's effects by blocking feedback that would otherwise limit its action. Together, they reduce appetite and increase feelings of fullness and energy.

Who it's for

Contrave is prescribed for adults with:

  • A BMI of 30 or higher, or
  • A BMI of 27 or higher with at least one weight-related condition (type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol)

It works best alongside diet and exercise changes.

Common side effects

People taking naltrexone-bupropion often report nausea, constipation, headache and insomnia. Nausea typically lessens over time as the body adjusts. Bupropion carries a risk of seizures at higher doses and may affect mood or increase blood pressure in some people.

Cost and access

Contrave is available by prescription only. Insurance coverage varies; many plans require prior authorisation or impose high copays. GoodRx and similar discount services can lower costs for uninsured patients.
