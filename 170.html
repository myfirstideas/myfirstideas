Wegovy Pills Cost Per Month

Wegovy oral tablets are priced based on dosage strength, ranging from $299 to $1,399 per month for uninsured patients.

Pricing by Dose

The cost varies depending on which strength your prescription requires:

  • Starting doses cost less than maintenance doses
  • Higher strength tablets carry higher price tags
  • Monthly supply includes all required injections for the month

Insurance and Coverage

Many insurance plans now cover Wegovy for weight loss in patients who meet specific criteria. Coverage varies by:

  • Insurance provider and plan type
  • Medical necessity documentation
  • Prior authorisation requirements
  • Individual deductible and copay amounts

Savings Programs

Novo Nordisk offers a patient assistance program that may reduce costs to as low as $99 per month for eligible patients. Check eligibility based on:

  • Household income thresholds
  • Insurance status
  • Prescription date requirements

Generic Alternatives

No generic version of Wegovy tablets currently exists. Patients seeking lower-cost options should discuss alternatives with their clinician.

Where to Buy

Wegovy tablets are available through:

  • Local pharmacies with a valid prescription
  • Telehealth providers who can prescribe and ship directly
  • Online pharmacies with prescription verification

Prices may vary by pharmacy and location.
