What is Nitrofurantoin?

Nitrofurantoin is an antibiotic used to treat uncomplicated urinary tract infections (UTIs), including cystitis and certain cases of pyelonephritis. It works by stopping bacteria from growing and multiplying in the urinary tract.

How It Works

Nitrofurantoin is a bactericidal antibiotic that concentrates in the urine. It interferes with bacterial cell wall synthesis, effectively killing common UTI-causing organisms like E. coli.

Common Dosing

Typical dosing for uncomplicated UTI is 100 mg twice daily for 5 to 7 days. Your prescriber may adjust based on kidney function and infection severity.

Side Effects

Common side effects include nausea, headache, and dizziness. Take it with food or milk to reduce stomach upset. More serious side effects are rare but can include peripheral neuropathy and hepatotoxicity with long-term use.

Who Should Avoid It

Nitrofurantoin is not suitable for people with severe kidney disease, G6PD deficiency, or those in late pregnancy. Tell your provider about any allergies or medical conditions.

Getting It Through Telehealth

Many telehealth providers can diagnose UTIs and prescribe nitrofurantoin after reviewing your symptoms and, if needed, urine test results. Same-day prescriptions are often available.
