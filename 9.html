GLP-1 telehealth that takes insurance

Getting a GLP-1 prescription through telehealth doesn't mean paying out of pocket. Many telemedicine providers now accept insurance, though coverage varies by plan and medication.

How insurance covers GLP-1 telehealth

Most major insurers cover GLP-1 medications when prescribed by a doctor for approved conditions. What changes with telehealth is the delivery method, not the coverage itself. Your insurance will typically cover the medication the same way it would if prescribed in person.

What affects your costs

Your out-of-pocket cost depends on three things: your plan's formulary tier for the medication, your deductible status, and whether the telehealth provider is in-network. Some insurers place newer GLP-1s on higher tiers, which means higher copays or coinsurance.

Finding in-network telehealth providers

Start by checking your insurance company's directory for telehealth providers who prescribe GLP-1 medications. Call your plan directly to confirm which providers participate and what your specific coverage looks like. Medication costs can vary widely between plans and even between the same medication on different tiers.

What to ask before booking

Confirm the provider accepts your specific insurance plan, verify which GLP-1 medications they prescribe, and ask about consultation fees. Some telehealth platforms charge a separate visit fee on top of medication costs; others bundle it into the price.
