What the Zepbound Savings Card Covers

Novo Nordisk's savings card for Zepbound (semaglutide) helps lower your out-of-pocket costs. The card works with or without insurance coverage.

With Insurance

If you have commercial insurance, the savings card typically caps your copay at a set amount per month, usually between 0 and 250 dollars depending on your plan. The card covers the difference between your copay and the full prescription price.

The card generally does not work with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance programs.

Without Insurance

Uninsured patients can use the savings card to access Zepbound at a reduced price. Novo Nordisk offers the card to uninsured patients who meet income eligibility requirements, though exact thresholds vary.

Key Limitations

Read the fine print before enrolling. Coverage exclusions include government insurance plans, patients already receiving Zepbound through manufacturer assistance programs, and those in certain states with specific regulations.

The savings card is not a guarantee of approval. Novo Nordisk reviews applications and can deny coverage based on eligibility criteria.

Next Steps

Check Novo Nordisk's official website for current terms, eligible dosages, and how to apply. Ask your prescriber's office if they can help with the application process.
