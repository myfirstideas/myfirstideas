GLP-1 Medications Now Available Online

GLP-1 receptor agonists, originally developed for type 2 diabetes, have become widely available through telehealth. Programs differ significantly in pricing, medication options, and support structures.

How Telehealth GLP-1 Programs Work

Most platforms follow a similar process: you complete a health questionnaire, connect with a clinician for assessment, receive a prescription if approved, and have medication shipped to your home. Ongoing check-ins monitor your progress and adjust dosing as needed.

Key Differences Between Programs

Costs range widely depending on whether the program includes compounded versions or brand-name medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide. Some charge monthly subscriptions; others charge per prescription. Insurance coverage varies: some programs accept it, others don't.

Questions to Ask

Before signing up, clarify: What's included in the base price? Will insurance be billed? What happens if you stop the medication? How often do you speak with a clinician? What's the refund policy?

Getting Started

Review program fees, medication options, and support policies before committing. Many programs offer initial consultations at reduced cost, letting you assess whether the service fits your needs and budget.
