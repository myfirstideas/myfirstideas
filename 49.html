What is NuvaRing?

NuvaRing is a flexible plastic contraceptive ring about two inches in diameter. You insert it into your vagina where it stays for three weeks, releasing a steady dose of the hormones etonogestrel and ethinyl estradiol to prevent pregnancy.

How it works

The ring sits in your vagina and releases hormones that:

  • Prevent your ovaries from releasing an egg
  • Thicken cervical mucus to block sperm
  • Thin the uterine lining

You insert a new ring every three weeks, then have a ring-free week when you'll typically get your period.

Effectiveness

NuvaRing is over 99 percent effective with perfect use. With typical use, effectiveness drops to about 91 percent because of user error, like forgetting to insert a new ring on time.

What to expect

After insertion, you may feel the ring, though most people don't notice it during daily activities or sex. Some experience:

  • Vaginal irritation or discharge
  • Headaches
  • Breast tenderness
  • Nausea

Side effects typically ease within the first few weeks.

Cost and access

NuvaRing typically costs between $0 and $200 per month without insurance. Most health plans cover it. You'll need a prescription from a doctor or nurse practitioner.
