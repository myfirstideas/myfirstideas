Telehealth Providers: Compare Services and Find the Right Fit

Telehealth lets you see a doctor, therapist or specialist without leaving home. But which provider should you choose?

What to Compare When Choosing a Telehealth Provider

Not all telehealth services are built the same. Before you sign up, check these factors:

  • Pricing and payment: What does a visit cost? Do they accept your insurance? Are there membership or subscription fees?
  • Specialties available: Do they offer care for your condition? Can you see a therapist, dermatologist or other specialist?
  • Wait times: How long until your first appointment? Can you get an urgent visit?
  • Technology: Is the platform easy to use? What devices does it work on?
  • Availability: What hours do they operate? Do they offer weekend or evening appointments?
  • Licensing and regulation: Are providers licensed in your state? What credentials do they hold?
  • Patient experience: What do real users say about their visits? How responsive is customer service?
