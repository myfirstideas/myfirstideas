Ozempic and Wegovy are the same drug with different labels and different prices.

Both contain semaglutide, a glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist that slows stomach emptying and signals fullness to the brain. The molecule is identical. The manufacturer is identical. The dosing schedule is identical.

What differs is the indication on the label.

Ozempic is labelled for type 2 diabetes. Wegovy is labelled for weight management in adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related conditions.

This label difference drives the price difference. Ozempic typically costs less than Wegovy for the same dose, even though you're getting the same medication.

Why the price gap exists

Manufacturers set prices based on the indication, the market they're targeting, and what insurance will cover. A diabetes indication has a different market dynamic than a weight management indication. Insurance coverage patterns differ. Negotiating power differs. The result is that Wegovy carries a premium over Ozempic for identical semaglutide.

Finding the cheaper option

If you have a type 2 diabetes diagnosis, Ozempic may be covered by insurance at a lower cost. Some people with obesity use this route to access semaglutide more affordably.

If you don't have diabetes, Wegovy is the approved option, though cost remains a barrier for many. Patient assistance programs, pharmacy discount cards, and generic semaglutide (when available) can reduce out-of-pocket costs.

Always discuss pricing and coverage options with your prescriber and pharmacist. The same molecule shouldn't cost different amounts based on your diagnosis, but it does, and knowing this matters for your wallet.
