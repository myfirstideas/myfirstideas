Your RatedByPatients Account

Your account is where you manage everything about your telehealth experience with us.

What You Can Do

  • View and edit your profile information
  • Save your favourite providers and clinicians for quick access
  • Track your appointment history
  • Manage your communication preferences
  • Update payment and insurance details
  • Control privacy settings

Getting Started

Creating an account takes minutes. You'll need a valid email address and a password to get started.

Privacy and Security

Your health information is protected. We use industry-standard encryption and comply with healthcare privacy regulations when you store sensitive data in your account.

Need Help?

If you can't access your account or have questions about your profile, our support team is ready to help.

Keep Your Information Current

Updating your details regularly ensures you get the most accurate information about providers and appointments that match your needs.
