What is Valacyclovir?

Valacyclovir is an antiviral medication that treats infections caused by herpes simplex virus (HSV-1 and HSV-2), varicella-zoster virus (which causes chickenpox and shingles), and Epstein-Barr virus.

How It Works

Valacyclovir is a prodrug, meaning your body converts it into acyclovir, the active form. Once converted, it stops the virus from replicating by blocking viral DNA polymerase.

Common Uses

Valacyclovir treats:

  • Cold sores (oral herpes)
  • Genital herpes
  • Shingles (herpes zoster)
  • Chickenpox
  • Mononucleosis caused by Epstein-Barr virus

It can be used to prevent outbreaks in people with recurrent herpes infections.

Dosage

Dosage depends on the condition being treated and ranges from 500 mg to 1,000 mg taken two to five times daily. Always follow your doctor's prescribed dosage.

Side Effects

Common side effects include headache, nausea, and abdominal pain. Serious but rare side effects can include kidney problems and neurological symptoms. Report any concerning symptoms to your healthcare provider.

Important Considerations

Tell your doctor if you have kidney disease, are pregnant, or take other medications. Valacyclovir works best when started early in an infection.
