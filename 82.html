What Rimegepant Does

Rimegepant is a calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) antagonist. It blocks CGRP, a molecule involved in migraine pain, and works differently from older migraine drugs like triptans.

Speed and Form

It comes as an oral tablet you dissolve under your tongue. Most people see relief within 30 to 120 minutes. You don't swallow it with water.

Who It's For

Rimegepant treats acute migraines in adults. You take it when a migraine starts, not as a daily preventative (though some people use it for prevention under medical guidance).

How It Compares

Unlike triptans, rimegepant does not narrow blood vessels. This matters if you have heart disease, high blood pressure, or stroke risk. It may work when triptans don't, or cause fewer side effects.

Side Effects

Common ones: nausea, throat irritation from the tablet. Most are mild. Serious side effects are rare.

Cost and Access

Rimegepant is a branded drug (Nurtec ODT). Insurance coverage varies. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about copay assistance programs.
