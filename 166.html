Oral GLP-1 Medications for Weight Loss: Pricing and Coverage

Oral GLP-1 receptor agonists have become a common option for weight management. Semaglutide tablets (marketed as Rybelsus for diabetes, used off-label for weight loss) and tirzepatide tablets represent the main oral options in this drug class.

What Oral GLP-1 Pills Cost

List prices run high. A month's supply of oral semaglutide typically costs $900 to $1,200 before insurance. Tirzepatide tablets similarly price between $1,000 and $1,300 per month at full retail.

These are manufacturer list prices. Your actual cost depends entirely on your insurance, your specific plan design, and whether you qualify for patient assistance programs.

Insurance Coverage Reality

Most commercial insurers now cover oral GLP-1s for type 2 diabetes. Coverage for weight loss (off-label use) is inconsistent. Some plans cover it; many require prior authorization; some exclude it entirely.

Medicare generally does not cover GLP-1s for weight loss alone. It covers them only when diabetes is also present.

State Medicaid programs vary widely. Some cover these drugs for weight loss; others restrict coverage to diabetes.

Finding Your Out-of-Pocket Cost

Contact your insurance directly and confirm:

  1. Is this drug on your plan's formulary?
  2. What tier is it (and what's your tier copay)?
  3. Does your plan apply a deductible first?
  4. Is prior authorization required?
  5. Are there quantity limits per month?

Patient assistance programs from manufacturers can reduce costs significantly if you qualify based on income.

Next Steps

Verify coverage before starting. Prices and coverage rules change frequently, and individual plan details matter far more than industry averages.
