Online Hair Loss Treatment: Hims vs Keeps

You can get prescription hair loss medication through several telehealth platforms. The main players are Hims and Keeps, but other options exist depending on what you need and what your insurance covers.

What These Services Offer

Online hair loss treatment typically includes:

  • Telemedicine consultations with doctors or nurse practitioners
  • Prescription medications like finasteride (Propecia) and minoxidil (Rogaine)
  • Combination treatments in some cases
  • Home delivery of medications

Hims vs Keeps: Key Differences

Both platforms connect you to licensed prescribers, but they differ in pricing models, medication selection, and how they handle consultations.

Hims offers a broad range of treatments and bundles options. Keeps focuses specifically on hair loss with a streamlined process. Price varies by medication and dosage, so compare what you actually need rather than assuming one platform is cheaper across the board.

Other Online Hair Loss Options

Beyond the two largest platforms, you can get prescription hair loss treatment through:

  • Direct-to-consumer telehealth apps with general practitioners
  • Your regular doctor via video visit
  • Online pharmacies that require a valid prescription
  • Dermatology-specific telemedicine services

What to Check Before You Choose

Compare these specifics:

  • Does your insurance cover the service and medications?
  • What medications does each platform offer?
  • What is the actual out-of-pocket cost for your prescription strength and dosage?
  • How quickly can you get a consultation?
  • What is the refill process?

Shopping by brand name alone costs you money. The medication matters more than the platform.
