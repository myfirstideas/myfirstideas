Ubrogepant is an oral medication that stops migraine pain by blocking calcitonin gene related peptide (CGRP) receptors in the brain. It's designed for acute migraine treatment, meaning you take it when a migraine starts rather than as a daily preventive.

How Ubrogepant Works

Migraine involves the release of CGRP, a protein that causes inflammation and pain signalling. Ubrogepant blocks CGRP receptors, interrupting this pain pathway. It's not a triptan and doesn't narrow blood vessels, making it an option for people who can't take triptans.

Dosing and Timing

The standard dose is 100 mg taken orally at the first sign of migraine. You can take a second dose 2 hours later if needed. The maximum is 200 mg per 24 hours.

Side Effects

Common side effects include nausea, drowsiness and dizziness. Most are mild. Serious side effects are rare but can include liver problems in people with existing liver disease.

Insurance and Availability

Ubrogepant (brand name Ubrelvy) typically requires prior authorisation from insurance. Many plans cover it after you've tried other migraine treatments. Copays range from $35 to $150 per prescription depending on your plan and whether a generic version is available.

Finding the Right Migraine Treatment

If you're considering ubrogepant, discuss with your doctor whether it suits your migraine pattern, other medications you take, and your insurance coverage.
