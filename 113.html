Cost Plus Drugs vs Amazon Pharmacy: Which Mail-Order Service Costs Less

Both Cost Plus Drugs and Amazon Pharmacy deliver medications to your door, but they work differently and save money in different ways.

How They Price Drugs

Cost Plus Drugs marks up generics by a flat 5 percent plus a $3 to $5 dispensing fee. Amazon Pharmacy uses standard pharmacy pricing tied to your insurance. Cost Plus has no membership fee; Amazon Pharmacy is free for Prime members.

Which Costs Less

Cost Plus typically wins on generics if you're uninsured or have a high deductible. Amazon Pharmacy often costs less if your insurance covers the drug and your copay is low. The math changes per prescription.

What You Can Actually Get

Amazon Pharmacy carries more brand-name drugs and has wider insurance network acceptance. Cost Plus focuses on generics and older medications. If you take a newer drug, Amazon Pharmacy is more likely to have it.

Speed and Convenience

Amazon offers faster delivery in some areas through Prime. Cost Plus takes longer but charges nothing for standard mail. Both let you manage refills online.

The Practical Choice

Use Cost Plus for cheap generics if you're price-sensitive and uninsured. Use Amazon Pharmacy if you have insurance and want brand-name coverage. Many people use both, depending on the prescription.

Next Steps

Get your current medication costs quoted at both services. The difference per year might surprise you.
