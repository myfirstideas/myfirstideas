What is Doxycycline?

Doxycycline is a broad-spectrum tetracycline antibiotic that treats a wide range of bacterial infections. It works by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis, stopping bacteria from multiplying.

Common Uses

Doxycycline treats:

  • Acne and rosacea
  • Respiratory tract infections
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Sexually transmitted infections including chlamydia and gonorrhoea
  • Malaria prevention and treatment
  • Lyme disease
  • Periodontitis and other dental infections
  • Anthrax exposure

How It Works

Doxycycline is a tetracycline antibiotic. It crosses cell membranes easily and concentrates in tissues, making it effective for infections in the skin, lungs, and urinary tract. For acne, it reduces bacterial populations and has mild anti-inflammatory effects.

Dosage

Dosage varies by indication, patient age, and renal function. Typical doses range from 50 mg to 200 mg per day in divided doses. Take doxycycline with a full glass of water and remain upright for 30 minutes to prevent oesophageal irritation.

Side Effects

Common side effects include:

  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Diarrhoea
  • Sun sensitivity (photosensitivity)
  • Vaginal yeast infections
  • Headache

Serious but rare side effects include severe allergic reactions, liver damage, and increased intracranial pressure.

Important Considerations

Avoid doxycycline if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or allergic to tetracyclines. Use sun protection during treatment. Tell your doctor about all medications you take, as doxycycline interacts with some drugs including warfarin and certain birth control pills.
