Price check: what telehealth visits actually cost

Telehealth visit costs range from $40 to $300 per appointment, depending on the provider, visit type, and whether you have insurance.

Without insurance

Uninsured telehealth visits typically cost:

  • Initial consultation: $75 to $150
  • Follow-up visit: $50 to $100
  • Urgent care visit: $99 to $199
  • Mental health session: $60 to $200

Some providers offer flat-rate pricing. Others charge by the minute or bundle visits into subscription plans.

With insurance

Covered telehealth visits usually cost the same copay as in-person visits, typically $20 to $50. Your deductible and coinsurance still apply.

Check your plan documents or call your insurer to confirm telehealth coverage before booking. Not all plans cover all visit types.

How to find transparent pricing

Before you book, the provider should tell you:

  • The exact cost of your visit
  • What's included (prescription, follow-up message, referral)
  • Cancellation fees
  • Whether they accept your insurance

If a provider won't quote a price upfront, look elsewhere. Price transparency is non-negotiable.

Red flags

  • No pricing listed on the website
  • Prices only available after you create an account
  • Hidden fees added at checkout
  • Surprise balance bills after insurance processing

Read the terms of service and privacy policy before you commit. Legitimate providers make this easy to find.
