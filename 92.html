TB-500 is a synthetic peptide derived from thymosin beta-4, a naturally occurring protein in the body. It's marketed primarily for tissue repair, wound healing, and recovery from injury.

How TB-500 Works

TB-500 is believed to work by promoting cell growth, reducing inflammation, and increasing blood vessel formation. The peptide may help repair damaged muscle and connective tissue, though most evidence comes from animal studies rather than human clinical trials.

Current Research Status

While TB-500 shows promise in laboratory and animal research for muscle recovery and wound healing, human studies remain limited. Most clinical data comes from small trials or anecdotal reports from athletes and fitness enthusiasts.

Regulatory and Legal Status

TB-500 is not approved by the FDA for any indication in the United States. It's not available through standard pharmaceutical channels or prescription. Some versions are sold as research chemicals or through online suppliers, though the quality, purity, and safety of these products cannot be guaranteed.

Common Uses

People use TB-500 off-label for:

  • Muscle injury recovery
  • Joint and tendon repair
  • General tissue healing
  • Athletic performance enhancement

Safety Considerations

Because TB-500 lacks FDA approval and robust human safety data, potential risks are not fully understood. Users should be aware that unregulated products may contain contaminants or inaccurate dosing.

If you're considering TB-500, speak with a healthcare provider about safer, evidence-based alternatives for your specific condition.
