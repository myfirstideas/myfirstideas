See Your Healthcare Costs Before You Book

Guessing what healthcare will cost wastes time and money. Our calculators show you actual prices so you can plan your budget and compare providers.

What You Can Calculate

Our tools cover the expenses that matter most: telehealth visit fees, medication costs, and out-of-pocket expenses based on your insurance plan. Enter your details once and see multiple scenarios without repeating yourself.

Why Numbers Matter More Than Promises

Providers quote prices in different ways. Some charge per visit. Others charge per month. Insurance plans have different copays and deductibles. Our calculators handle the math so you don't have to.

How to Use These Tools

Select the calculator that matches what you're trying to budget for. Enter your insurance information if you have it, or see uninsured prices. The tool shows you what similar care costs across providers so you can compare apples to apples.

Get the Full Picture

Price is only part of your decision. Use these numbers alongside provider ratings, availability, and what patients actually report about their care. Money and quality both matter.
